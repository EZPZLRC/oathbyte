OathByte — Privacy Policy

OathByte is a free multiplayer action RPG. There are no accounts and no sign-in: you pick a hero name and play.

What stays on your device

Your heroes, gear, stash, settings and progress are stored in your device's browser storage (localStorage) inside the app. They never leave your device except as described below. Deleting the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes your heroes.

What goes to the game server

OathByte is an online game. When you play, the app connects to the OathByte game server (oathbyte.onrender.com) over an encrypted connection so you can play in a shared world with other players. To run that shared world, the server necessarily receives:

  • Your chosen hero name, hero class and level, so other players can see who you are and party up with you.
  • Your chat messages (up to 140 characters each), which are shown to other players in your party or world. Do not share personal information in chat.
  • Items you give to another player, and party invitations.
  • Your progress toward shared goals: the seasonal leaderboard records hero name, class, level and floors cleared; the world-boss count records how many times the shared world boss has been defeated and who hit it.

Your connection address is used only in memory while you are connected — to verify Founder Pack codes — and is not saved to disk.

What we do not collect

OathByte has no accounts, so we do not ask for or store your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment details. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics SDKs. The optional Founder Pack is bought outside the app (on Ko-fi); the game only checks the code you type in, and the check does not share any personal information.

Other players

Hero names, chat messages and leaderboard entries are visible to other players. Choose a hero name you are comfortable with strangers seeing.

Children

OathByte is a fantasy combat game and is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please play with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and the "last updated" date below will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: contact the developer through the support link on the Google Play listing.
